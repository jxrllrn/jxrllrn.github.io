A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A little clarity.

Is HINAY a real skincare product?＋

HINAY is a fictional brand created for a portfolio campaign. No physical product is being sold, and no formulation or skincare performance is claimed.

What is this website for?＋

It is a brand-experience showcase: a place to explore the concept, product visuals, campaign design, and creative process. It demonstrates how those elements can work together on a website.

How was the campaign made?＋

ChatGPT/Codex developed the strategy, copy, imagery and layouts at Jarrell’s request. The product images were generated with a shared bottle reference; typography and campaign layouts were composed separately. The process page explains the work and its limits.

Where are the files and videos?＋

The creative kit contains artwork, editable layouts, copy, brand assets and the two finished MP4 edits. You can watch the films here or download them. The original video scripts and end cards remain included.
