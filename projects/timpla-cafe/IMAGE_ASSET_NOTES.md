# Product Image Notes

The product visuals are original AI-generated assets made for this fictional portfolio brand. They use a consistent transparent plastic cup, soft premium studio lighting, a centered product composition, and Timpla Café's original circular espresso badge with a cream `T`. The supplied menu screenshot was used only as a reference for catalog clarity and product-card presentation; its branding and layout were not copied.

## Prompt direction used

Each prompt requested one isolated, photorealistic menu product on a transparent background with no text other than the Timpla badge. The shared art direction was then adapted to the actual drink or pastry:

- Timpla Latte: iced espresso, muscovado ribbons, creamy milk
- Ube Cloud Latte: iced latte with purple ube cream and coconut cloud
- Barako Brown Sugar: dark barako coffee with brown-sugar milk marbling
- Calamansi Cold Brew: cold brew with bright calamansi citrus accents
- Tablea Mocha: espresso with rich local tablea chocolate
- Salted Muscovado Latte: layered latte with salted muscovado cream
- Pandan Coco Matcha: green matcha with pandan and coconut milk
- Mango Sago Fizz: golden mango soda with visible mini sago
- Classic Ensaymada: soft butter pastry with sugar and grated cheese
